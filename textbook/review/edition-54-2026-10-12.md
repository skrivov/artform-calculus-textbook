## Corrections needed in the book's claims about verification

1. **Qualify “every crossing” and the record guarantee.** Book source
   `chapters/01-foundations.tex:5194-5196` says every crossing into a form or
   presentation passes a gate. Ordinary form-to-presentation dependencies
   are allowed: `demand form presentation = free`, and the checked chess
   construction has the free edge `board -> addressing`.
   `Construction.crosses_gate` requires endpoint demand **gated**. It proves
   existence of a gated edge and reachability of its target, in a graph that
   interprets that grade as recorded use. The library has no operational gate
   syntax or event/payload storage theorem. Actual contraction/record
   persistence belongs to the paper-only calculus. Say that Lean checks the
   firewall and mediation of dependencies required to be gated in this graph
   model; attribute operational record retention to the paper proof.
2. **State the mechanization boundary.** Book source `frontmatter.tex:47-53`
   can be read as saying all book theorems and the paper's type theory are
   mechanized. Two book statements still have no Lean statement; the status
   table also records returned translations and pending proofs. The core
   dependent calculus is explicitly unmechanized, and the paper's role-count
   interpretation is returned by this review. Describe an ongoing
   formalization and selected checked results of the role model. Section
   1.11's reference to Lean checking the account of roles should be read with
   the same explicit boundary, not as verification of the combined calculus.

These are requests for the book's own editing/release process. This review
does not edit the book. They do not revoke the unchanged numbered translations
or prevent this repository from following the book it actually accompanies.
