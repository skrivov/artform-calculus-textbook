\medskip
{\large An introduction to Lean, and to the book's theorems in Lean\par}
\end{center}
\bigskip

\noindent Part~I explains the basics of Lean, a programming language in which
proofs are checked by the computer. Part~II writes the constructions of
Chapter~1 of \emph{Artform Calculus} in Lean. Part~III introduces the
repository that holds this guide, where the book's numbered statements are
written in Lean with Mathlib, with their proof and review status recorded,
and reads four of them closely.
References such as Section~1.4, Figure~1.9 or Definition~1.5 point into the
book; their numbers are those of its \bookedition. Every listing is cut from
files that Lean~\leanversion{} accepts as a whole: two files in core Lean for
Parts~I and~II, and for Part~III a file that imports the repository's
library, and the library itself.
