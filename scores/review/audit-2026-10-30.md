Reviewed baseline: `fec7898475642ca74e829911ab7d71e3a4473df8`.
Scope: the 63 entries submitted through `scores/STATUS.md`, their Lean
statements, reachable definitions, proofs, examples, and the nine dedicated

`REVIEW-HANDOFF.md` is covered only where it concerns those Lean results.

## Returned entries

### J1. R4: the balanced word and the product of laws differ

[Basic.lean](../ArtformScores/Basic.lean), line 98 (`balanced`), defines
`realize (whitehead u) * (realize (whitehead 1))⁻¹`, a product of laws.
[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), `balancedRule`, correctly
proves that this product is `diag(u,u⁻¹)` and is multiplicative.

The ledger's §3 instead introduces rules on scores and writes the word
`h(u) = w(u) w(1)⁻¹`. Definition 6 reverses multiplication when realizing a
word. That word therefore realizes `W(1)⁻¹ W(u) = diag(u⁻¹,u)`.
`JudgeChecks.lean` verifies this for `u = 2` over the rationals, obtaining
`diag(1/2,2)`. Thus the definition silently chooses a product of laws in place
of realizing the displayed score word.

Required resolution: distinguish the law product from the score, or correct
the time-order score to `w(1)⁻¹ w(u)`, and connect the chosen score to its law.
Keep the valid matrix theorem; do not change its diagonal to an incorrect one.
This also explains the reversed concatenation used in the valid six-step
existence proof for T2:group.

### J2. R2: the source score commutator has the wrong sign

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 676, and
[Steinberg.lean](../ArtformScores/Steinberg.lean), `steinbergRules'`, correctly
proves the positive matrix commutator and both explicit time-order forms.
The word `e_ij(f), e_jk(g), e_ij(-f), e_jk(-g)` realizes `e_ik(-fg)`.
The positive result belongs to the reverse list
`e_jk(-g), e_ij(-f), e_jk(g), e_ij(f)`.

The ledger still prints the positive commutator among its score relations.
Its later “In Lean” paragraph discloses the sign issue, but does not repair
that defining relation. `JudgeChecks.lean` separates the two laws over the
rationals on three channels with `f = g = 1`.

Required resolution: specify that the bracket is a matrix-law bracket, or
correct the relation on time-order words. The Lean identities are sound;
this is a return of fidelity to the unrepaired source rule.

### J3. T12:reading: invariance is only one part of the submitted claim

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 207 (`readingDetermines`),
asserts only that equal phase and reading give equal output at every tick
from rest. [Readings.lean](../ArtformScores/Readings.lean) proves that implication
by Cayley–Hamilton. It has no output-to-reading converse and no stated closed
form. Both omissions are acknowledged by the Phase 1 handoff.

The submitted manifest quotation and finding 057, S3.6(1), claim a complete
invariant and the displayed trigonometric expression. Merely documenting the
omission does not formalize that claim. Required resolution: retain the
invariance lemma and state/prove the missing observational converse, with
the output preparation quantified so that zero initial data cannot make it
vacuous. Supply the closed form in its appropriate setting, including its
boundary cases, or obtain an explicit change to the claimed source scope.
The algebraic invariance lemma is accepted as a correct intermediate result.

### J4. T14: the cancellation iff needs precise quantification

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 239 (`callerCut`), proves the
two cuts, the common trace and the exact mean-cut formula. Its docstring and
manifest also promise cancellation exactly for a fair caller. There is no
such iff in the proposition.

At a single degenerate mode, the unrestricted iff would be false: at `h = 0`,
a caller of weight `1/4` has zero mean cut. Equal pulls give another degeneracy.
The compiled review witness checks the first case. This does not refute the
proved formula or the intended claim about cancelling the scheme's cut
uniformly over all pulls.

Required resolution: formulate cancellation uniformly over pulls with a
nonzero step, or state the iff under `h ≠ 0` and `gx ≠ gy`. The formula already
provides the algebraic core. Numerical orders, stochastic convergence and
spectral gaps remain outside the submitted Lean scope.

### J5. T2:group: the field case does not cover the other submitted dials

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 707 (`indexGroup`), and
[Steinberg.lean](../ArtformScores/Steinberg.lean) establish the correct quotient
criterion over a field's Laurent ring: the elementary group is the determinant
kernel and the determinant is onto. These two assertions are an acceptable
encoding of the quotient isomorphism. The unit-monomial characterization and
six-step balanced mover are also correct there.

The ledger states its group claim in the setting including `Z/p^k` and `Z/n`;
the submitted translation restricts the whole statement to fields. The reason
in the handoff addresses the monomial description of units, not the quotient
claim. For example, `1 + 7x` is a unit over `(Z/49)[x,x⁻¹]`, with inverse
`1 - 7x`, and is not a monomial. This disproves that wider unit description,
not the determinant quotient theorem.

Required resolution: separate the quotient statement for all the positive
dials of T1 from the monomial-unit theorem over fields. Preserve the current
field result and record the source correction for units. This review does not
silently treat the field restriction as completion of the full submitted T2.

### J6. T11: the normalization/gluing claim is missing, and the source needs a condition

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 727 (`faceAndLink`), proves
intertwining by `diag(1,D*)`, the entry identities, the zero-mode values and
`D* I_face ⊆ I_link ⊆ I_face`. This formulation correctly avoids treating `D*`
as a unit of the Laurent ring. It does not assert the image under the
normalization/gluing map claimed by T11 and finding 064, S3.10.

There is also a source problem: S3.10 says the face form of *any* weighted
score is glued and uses `T_face(1) = K(κ)` in its proof. The actual formula is
`K(κ × totalKick w)`. An empty score, or cancelling kick weights, has the
identity there, even when `κ ≠ 0`. The empty-score witness compiles. The Lean
formula exposes this error correctly.

Required resolution: state the needed nonzero total-kick condition where
gluedness is claimed, and prove the appropriate normalization/module-class
bridge. A general Picard-group statement also needs its domain clarified for
noninvertible ideals. The special leapfrog cases proved in T8 do not prove the
general-phase T11 claim. Keep the intertwining and ideal-inclusion lemmas.

### J7. T13:platonic: counts do not identify the named solids

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 550 (`platonicSolids`), gives
only the group orders and three `(V,E,F)` count triples. It mentions neither
an incidence isomorphism to the tetrahedron, octahedron and icosahedron nor a
uniqueness theorem identifying them. `regularMap` supplies useful determinant
incidences and regularity data, but the submitted proposition does not provide
the final identification with the named structures.

Required resolution: supply explicit graph/incidence isomorphisms or a precise
classification theorem applied to these maps. A spatial embedding is not
required. The numeric counts themselves are accepted as correct. The separately
proved `PSL(2,5) ≅ A5` does not by itself identify the twelve-vertex map.

### J8. T8:curve: point formulas do not state the whole geometric theorem

[Statements.lean](../ArtformScores/Statements.lean), line 806 (`leapfrogCurve`), asserts
the rational parametrization, its inverse off `x = 1`, the condition `s² = q`
over that fibre, and the uniqueness of `λ = 1` on that fibre. These identities
are correct. It does not assert that this is the normalization, that the
singularity is an ordinary node, or that there are no other singularities.
The handoff explicitly acknowledges the missing singularity claim.

Required resolution: add the algebraic/geometric bridge, with characteristic
not two for the ordinary-node conclusion and the two geometric branches.
Equations over arbitrary fields are useful lemmas, but in characteristic two
`s² = q` does not describe two distinct geometric branches. The classification
proof's use of a ring named `Norm` is not itself a stated normalization theorem.
This return does not invalidate the independently stated processing-class
classification or its counts.

### J9. T15:back-step: no positive non-vacuity witness is supplied

[BackSteps.lean](../ArtformScores/BackSteps.lean), `oneBackStep`, is a faithful
and stronger translation of finding 043, Lemma 3: a permutation suffices; it
need not be one Hamiltonian cycle. The proof uses the predecessor/coset argument
and has the expected type and allowed axioms.

Its `Non-vacuity` section (line 437), however, proves that the proposed `Z/6` example
cannot meet the one-back-step hypotheses. A no-go application is useful but
is not the positive witness required by AGENTS rule 6. No positive witness for
this implication is supplied by that package.

Required resolution: add an explicit permutation satisfying the forward-step
hypotheses and an actual backward branch. A small candidate is the cycle
`0 → 1 → 3 → 2 → 4 → 0` on the additive `Z/5` with forward steps `+1,+2`:
only `3 → 2` is backward. Prove the conditions, rather than assuming the run.
This is a return for the proof package, not an objection to Lemma 3's translation.

## Decisions on the other highlighted points

- **Syntax and realizer.** Lists with negative stencils represent every signed
  score, so the list implementation is acceptable for these law identities.
  `realize_append` has the required reversed multiplication. The one-mode
  presentation's homomorphism uses conventional matrix products; its explicit
  `X^-3 Y` example and palindromic Whitehead word fix the ordering. J1/J2 identify
  the places where the source fails to keep the two conventions distinct.
- **T1.** Polynomial degree after a shift is a valid well-founded descent;
  minimal Laurent width need not be the induction measure. Nilpotence in the
  reduction kernel is proved coefficientwise; CRT idempotents isolate the
  factors. No corner-unit or nonzero-entry hypothesis is added to T1.
- **T4.** Permutation tables satisfying relators alone would not give the needed
  upper bound. The current proof does more: `Coset.good_of_check` inducts on
  strictly earlier edge justifications, and `cover_of_check` proves coverage
  by the represented cosets. `complete_of_cert` then excludes a nontrivial
  kernel by the bottom rows and `X^p = 1`. The four certificates instantiate
  that soundness theorem. The earlier plan's lower-bound mistake is repaired.
- **R6.** The group-ring inclusion defines the scalar action; the unique coset
  representatives yield the required basis. The checkerboard's four black
  coefficients and constant-term obstruction justify the plane instance.
  Neither the rounded integer half-move nor a converse to the obstruction is
  claimed by the approved entries.
- **T7.** `SameClass` uses non-zero-divisor factors and covers noninvertible
  ideals, as the nodal setting requires. The discriminant condition is the
  fraction-field nonsquare condition written by clearing denominators. Its
  characteristic-two vacuity does not drop a source case: finding 057 already
  works in characteristic different from two. Surjectivity uses the PID
  hypothesis, and `Euclid.lean` supplies that instance for the stencil ring.
- **T15.** Extents are single-cycle permutations, not unchecked words.
  `FewestBack` includes existence as well as a lower bound. Back-steps are
  failures of both forward choices; the models establish genuine distinct
  steps where their counts require them. Composite `PSLZ` consistently means
  the quotient by `{±1}`, matching the ledger's code, rather than automatically
  the whole-center quotient. The prime-field Fibonacci result uses Mathlib's
  center quotient, where the distinction disappears.
- **T13.** The subgroup definitions, exact finite orders, infinite indices,
  ping-pong freeness and three normal-closure presentations match Theorem Q.
  The added presentations close the original handoff's omission. The projective
  action of the actual two-square permutations, its forced labelling and its
  `S5`/`A5` identifications are substantive theorems, independent of J7.
- **T8.** The existential classification map is sufficiently pinned. Every
  unit `a+br` of the two-dimensional fibre is either a nonzero scalar or a
  scalar times `y+r`; the former has `theta = 1`. Hilbert-90 surjectivity and
  the explicit `gluedLaw` values cover the glued quotient, and `ungluedLaw`
  fixes the extra value. Equality iff processing then prevents an arbitrary
  relabelling of unrepresented classes. The magic-step normal forms and
  `ofPair` likewise pin that map; the two crossing tests distinguish its three
  exceptional values. The quotient in the count statements is of *all* laws
  of the specified phase, not just a sampled stencil family.
- **T12 correction.** The square-determinant reversal criterion is stronger
  than mere reversal-fixedness. The three alternatives in
  `leapfrogPalindromes` express the exact existence statement, including the
  kappa representative. `palindromeGap` proves actual processing into the
  inverse and nonexistence of every equal-diagonal representative at each of
  the fourteen pairs. Exhaustiveness of that list up to 41 is not approved.
- **Examples.** Most packages have concrete, verified running examples.
  `LeapfrogExamples.lean` contains substantial positive examples for the T8
  and leapfrog-palindrome results; accepting their substance does not certify
  literal compliance with rule 6's placement “at the end of its proof file”.
  Add local `Non-vacuity` references/examples in those proof files. Similarly,
  `Extents.lean` proves only that the rank condition permits `p = 7`; an actual
  positive extent is proved in `ExtentTable.some7`. That supplies the missing
  substantive existence evidence for the Fibonacci implication. J9 lacks such
  a positive one-back-step witness. These placement issues remain recorded
  package work; no whole-repository compliance approval is given here.
