of `whitehead 1`, followed by `whitehead u`, so its law is precisely
`W(u) W(1)⁻¹`. `balancedRule` retains the diagonal and multiplicativity
conclusions and adds the missing equality between this score and that law.
The ledger's defining word agrees. The independent rational witness gives
`diag(2,1/2)`; the preserved old-order witness still gives `diag(1/2,2)`.
The distinction that caused J1 is resolved explicitly.

### J7: T13:platonic, incidence rather than counts

`PrimitiveGraphModel` asserts primitive representatives, existence and
uniqueness for every primitive state modulo sign, and adjacency iff
determinant is `±1`, for every pair. These clauses give a bijection on
vertices and on edges, beyond the preserved counts. The three target
graphs are `K4`, the complete tripartite graph with opposite pairs, and
two pentagonal rings with two poles and staggered cross edges, respectively.
These are the tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral graphs.

The existing `triangle_is_face` theorem composes with the graph models:
when an initial determinant is `−1`, negating that representative makes it
`1` without changing its class. This composition is checked independently
in Lean. Conversely, a source face `{[u],[v],[u+v]}` has all three edges.
Independent enumeration constructs the actual source faces from determinant
1 pairs and finds exactly all target triangles: 4, 8 and 20. Thus the
identifications preserve the required incidence structures. A spatial
embedding is unnecessary for the submitted claim.

### J8: T8:curve, normalization and the node

The source is the actual quotient `S[λ]/(λ²−tλ+1)`, with `S=F[x,x⁻¹]`.
The new map is injective and sends the actual root to `1+(x−1)s`.
Every element of the specified localized line is integral over that source
and is a fraction of source elements, with nonzero denominator `x−1`.
The target is a normal domain. These are sufficient normalization data in
their common fraction field: anything integral over the source is integral
over the target, and the target is integrally closed. This is stronger than
a parametrization on field-valued points.

The displayed singular equations are exactly the curve equation and its two
partial derivatives after multiplying by the unit `x`. Their common torus
zero is `(1,1)`. Translation gives quadratic term `v²−q u²`; `q≠0` and
characteristic not 2 make its tangent polynomial separable. Once a square
root is present, the two factors are explicitly distinct. The proof is
generic over fields, so it also applies after passage to an algebraic
closure; it does not require the node to split over the initial field.
The original point formulas and exceptional fibre remain a conjunct.
The rational `q=2` example witnesses the integral birational construction.
