## Relation to existing work

Discrete variational constraint analysis, including dependence on the initial
and final evolution steps, is established work; F1 is a checked application
to the particular retained native source, not a new general constraint theory.
See Dittrich and Hoehn,
[Constraint analysis for variational discrete systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4294).
Constrained discrete quantization and the need to specify projectors and
physical state spaces also predate this reference. See Hoehn,
[Quantization of systems with temporally varying discretization I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6062).
No equivalence of F2 with that physical quantization is proved.

Recent work explicitly relates second-order constraints and linearization
stability to perturbative quantum gravity, including boundary qualifications.
See De Vuyst, Eccles, Hoehn and Kirklin,
[Linearization (in)stabilities and crossed products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19931v3)
(JHEP2025,211). This supports investigating nonlinear admission before a
quantum interpretation; it does not identify our finite isotropic force with
a continuum Taub charge, a crossed-product algebra or a graviton constraint.
That relevance is an inference, not a correspondence theorem. General
linearization-instability and Gaussian mechanisms are prior art; no priority
claim is made for them.
