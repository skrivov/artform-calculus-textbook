## Why continuing generic formalization is no longer the best physical test

The original compact score already has exact non-Abelian Gauss preservation,
a genus-two orbit dictionary and standard handle-twist actions. The
geometric fixture showed that a full kick can leave the matched positive-
curvature sector; preserving an intrinsic tuple with a free connection
does not choose a native gravitational Hamiltonian. The native audit then
showed why matching only rotational connection coordinates is insufficient.
The productive change was deriving actual native boundary data. It yielded
new source-specific constraints, contacts and conditioning information.

At this point the framework is materially useful as a verified compiler
and adverse-test suite for one frozen native quadratic research candidate.
Further abstract AMM, Ward, Schur or Gaussian theorems alone would not
choose the missing physical law, observable algebra, state or measure.
That is the reason to finish this bounded campaign with the concrete
native tools and an honest handoff, rather than count more generic
identities as quantum-gravity progress.

## What a later application must demonstrate

A physical claim needs a source-selected map of fields, link variables and
boundary momenta, its complete retained equations and sources, a specified
geometric/admissible domain, and a same-domain improvement on an actual
unresolved native problem. A quantum claim additionally needs its measure,
amplitude, constraints, state and common domains. Earlier P179/P189/P190
and M162/common-Weyl reference/domain qualifications remain untouched.

The current campaign earns four bounded research-tool bundles and zero
selected physical applications. All eight physical directions remain
open. Decision12 and the old C/D stage gates are unchanged. These are
limits on what has been shown, not universal impossibility theorems or a
claim that a new physical construction cannot succeed.
