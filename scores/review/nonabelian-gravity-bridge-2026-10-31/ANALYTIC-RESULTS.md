closedness of the restricted form, or nondegeneracy of its reduced form.

## The geometric control changes the next constructor contract

Every present star has valence2 or3. The new Lean preflight proves that none
has valence4. The present two-face canvas cannot simply be relabelled as a
four-valent tetrahedral closure problem.

There is also an admitted unit-quaternion example:

```text
U=(i,1,1,1,1,1,1), P=(i,i,i,i,1,1,1).
```

Both this state and its complete face0 kick satisfy all six Gauss equations;
all their links and impulses have unit norm. The kick changes P0 from i
to -1, with real parts0 and-1. Their conjugacy classes differ. Thus a
proposed fixed-conjugacy-class domain needs its own preservation proof;
Gauss plus the ambient form identity does not supply it. This example is
not asserted to reconstruct a nondegenerate tetrahedron and does not rule
out a domain with evolving face areas.

A future C01 contract must therefore supply an actual four-valent canvas,
declare whether face areas evolve, fix curvature scale and branches, and
give reconstruction and domain-escape tests. The curved reconstruction
theorem requires geometric holonomy interpretation, a fixed simple path,
consistent orientation and a nonzero Gram determinant. Closure alone does
not discharge those conditions. [Haggard, Han and Riello, Theorem1](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.03053v1)
