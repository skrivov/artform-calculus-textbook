# Prior art and the contribution being tested

The novelty target is a checked application to this native source. The
following established constructions are not counted as discoveries.

Marsden and West's [discrete mechanics review](https://www.cds.caltech.edu/~marsden/bib/2001/09-MaWe2001/MaWe2001.pdf)
develops discrete Legendre maps and the variational construction of
symplectic evolution. Dittrich and Hoehn's
[constraint analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4294)
addresses singular evolving phase spaces; a null direction does not by
itself establish a gauge symmetry. Hoehn's
[quadratic-action classification](https://arxiv.org/html/1407.6641v3)
treats effective actions, pre/post constraints, region-dependent reduced
spaces, quantum measures and composition. Our source-specific matrices,
affine boundary representative, retained source conditions and extension
counterexamples must provide the value beyond those general results.

Freidel and Speziale's
[twisted geometries](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2748)
already parametrize intrinsic and extrinsic data in the SU2 graph phase
space. Dupuis, Freidel, Livine and Speziale's
[Lorentzian simplicity constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.5274)
construct a Lorentz-algebra twistor phase space and constraints with a
polyhedral interpretation. These are relevant comparisons for a possible
compact/native bridge. Merely replacing SU2 by a Lorentz group, adding
boosts or naming a twist an extrinsic curvature is not a new physical
construction. The chosen native action, its boundary covectors and its
constraints still require a faithful dictionary. The
[original real-connection construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9410014)
likewise does not license identifying an arbitrary compact score variable
with an Ashtekar-Barbero connection.

The frozen native programme already contains stronger force-Hessian
range results and selected reference Weyl/Fock constructions. Its P189,
P190 and later pinned M162/common-Weyl qualifications must remain. The
new temporal mixed boundary operator is a different operator, so its
actual source binding and conditioning are a legitimate new test. The
small-divisor mechanism, Schur identity and abstract quantization
machinery are established mathematics.

No search can prove universal priority or exhaust all possible field
maps. The operational decision is narrower: pursue the exact native
boundary admission, source compatibility and topology questions; record
which physical selections remain absent; do not rename known kinematics
or a reference representation as a solution to quantum gravity.
