Checked31October2026 against primary papers:

- Dittrich and Hoehn, *Constraint analysis for variational discrete systems*
  (2013), https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4294 : singular variational matching,
  pre/post constraints and their different roles are prior art. Rational
  symmetric congruence and complete linear-system source guards are standard

- Altas and Tekin, *Linearization Instability for Generic Gravity in AdS*
  (2017), https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10234 : higher-order constraints on
  linearized solutions and failure of linearized solutions to extend are
  prior art. Altas and Tekin, *Second Order Perturbation Theory in General
  Relativity: Taub Charges as Integral Constraints* (2019),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11982 : a concrete second-order obstruction in
  gravity is also prior art. Our prospective finite native lapse-force test
  must not be described as discovery of linearization instability or a
  continuum Taub charge.

and formal all-order bulk constructions. The follow-up tests arbitrary
finite-boundary compatibility for the same source, rather than rebranding
those constructions as new results. No priority claim or absence-of-prior-art
claim follows from this bounded search. Quantum Gaussian/Fresnel integration
and signature phases are standard; any value must lie in full native source,
measure, constraint and endpoint binding.
